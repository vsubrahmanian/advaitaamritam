_asaktaṁ sarvabhṛc-caiva nirguṇaṁ guṇa-bhoktṛ ca ||_

- Source: Bhagavad Gītā
- Location: Chapter 13, Verse 15 (also verse 14 in some recensions)
- Context / Speaker: Bhagavān Śrī Kṛṣṇa expounding the nature of the Supreme Knower (Jñeya / Brahman) to Arjuna

*Meaning*
Shining through the functions of all the senses, yet completely devoid of all senses; unattached, yet sustaining everything; free from the qualities of nature, yet the experiencer of all qualities.

*Reflection*
The eye sees, but it cannot see the light that lends it vision. You hear, touch, and think, yet that which allows you to experience is itself free from thoughts and sensations. The divine does not stand outside creation as a distant ruler; it is the silent, pure presence right now, unattached to the worldly play yet animating every breath you take.
