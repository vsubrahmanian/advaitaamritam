*Meaning*
You abide eternally at the root foundation of all existence. You embody the three powers of creation, sustenance, and dissolution; yogis meditate upon You without ceasing. You alone are Brahmā, You are Viṣṇu, You are Rudra, You are Indra, and You are Agni.

*Reflection*
Before any deity is given a name or form, there is the fundamental ground of Being upon which all experience arises. The elephant-headed lord is celebrated not merely as a remover of worldly obstacles, but as that very primordial ground—the silent witness within which creator, preserver, and all cosmic forces are simply expressions of the one Self. When you find stillness at the root of your own awareness, every divine form is realized as your own true nature.
